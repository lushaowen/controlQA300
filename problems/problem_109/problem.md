## Subproblems
1. Express the sinusoidal motor torque and constant gravitational torque in the Laplace domain.
2. Recall the transfer functions from P3.63 relating torque inputs to elevator velocity.
3. Compute the Laplace-domain expression for the elevator’s linear velocity.
4. Perform partial fraction expansion for the sinusoidal-input term.
5. Perform partial fraction expansion for the constant-input term.
6. Combine the results to obtain a complete expression for \( V_1(s) \).
7. Compute the inverse Laplace transform to find \( v_1(t) \).
8. Identify the transient component of the response.
9. Identify the steady-state component of the response.

## Additional Information
- The elevator model is linear and time-invariant.
- Zero initial conditions are assumed.
- The gravitational torque is treated as a constant disturbance input.
- Parameters \( \alpha \) and \( \beta \) are defined as in P3.63.
- Complex exponential and trigonometric Laplace transform pairs may be used.

## Constraints
- Use Laplace-domain methods consistently.
- Clearly distinguish between transient and steady-state terms.
- Assume all system parameters are positive.
- Final expressions should be simplified and physically interpretable.
