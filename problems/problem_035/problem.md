# Problem: Step Response of an RC Circuit

## Problem Description
Consider the \( {RC} \) -circuit where \( R = 1\mathrm{M}\Omega \) and \( C = {10\mu }\mathrm{F} \) . Assuming zero initial conditions, sketch the capacitor’s voltage, \( {v}_{C}\left( t\right) \) , when a constant voltage \( v\left( t\right)  = {10}\mathrm{\;V}, t \geq  0 \) , is applied to the circuit. Sketch also the circuit current \( i\left( t\right) \) .


## Tasks

1. Solve the governing differential equation for the capacitor voltage \( v_C(t) \).
2. Identify the time constant of the circuit.
3. Sketch the capacitor voltage \( v_C(t) \).
4. Compute the circuit current \( i(t) \).
5. Sketch the current response \( i(t) \).
6. Interpret the physical meaning of the results.

---

## Additional Information

- The governing equation of the circuit is:
  \[
  RC\,\dot{v}_C + v_C = v(t).
  \]
- All elements are ideal and linear.
- The voltage source is a step input.

---

## Constraints

- Assume zero initial conditions.
- Express results in terms of physical parameters.
- Clearly indicate steady-state and transient behavior.
- Sketches may be qualitative but must show correct trends.
