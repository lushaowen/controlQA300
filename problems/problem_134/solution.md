# Solution

## Method

Since the zeros of \( \widehat{K} \) are the poles of \( G \), the product of
\( \widehat{K} \) and \( e^{-s\tau} G \)
will perform a complete cancelation of the poles of \( G \). Therefore,
\( G \) must be asymptotically stable for internal stability.

## Teaching Points

1. Difference between input–output stability and internal stability
2. Risks associated with pole-zero cancellation
3. Structural properties of Smith predictor controllers
4. Role of plant stability in model-based control
5. Hidden unstable modes in feedback interconnections
