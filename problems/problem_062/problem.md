## Problem Description
Change variables under the Laplace integral to prove the time-shift property in Table 3.2.

## Subproblems

1. Write the Laplace transform of the time-shifted function using the definition.
2. Perform an appropriate change of variables in the integral.
3. Simplify the exponential term after substitution.
4. Identify the resulting expression as a Laplace transform.
5. Interpret the effect of time delay in the Laplace domain.

## Additional Information

- The unit step function ensures causality of the time-shifted signal.
- Assume all integrals converge.
- Let \( s = \beta + j\gamma \) with \( \beta \) sufficiently large.

## Constraints

- The derivation must begin from the integral definition of the Laplace transform.
- Variable substitution must be clearly shown.
- No use of transform tables.
- The final result must be expressed in closed form.
