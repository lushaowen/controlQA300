# Problem

## Problem Description
 Assume that the function \( f \) has exponential growth. Prove the integration property in Table 3.2. Hint: Evaluate the integral in \( \mathcal{L}\left\{  {{\int }_{{0}^{ - }}^{t}f\left( \tau \right) {d\tau }}\right\} \) by parts.
![alt text](images\image.png)
## Subproblems

1. Write the Laplace transform of the integral \( \int_{0^-}^{t} f(\tau)\, d\tau \) using its definition.
2. Apply integration by parts to the resulting integral.
3. Identify the boundary term that arises from integration by parts.
4. Use the exponential growth condition on \( f(t) \) to show that the boundary term vanishes.
5. Conclude the integration property of the Laplace transform.

## Additional Information

- The lower limit \( 0^- \) allows for possible impulse behavior at \( t = 0 \).
- Assume \( s = \beta + j\gamma \) with \( \beta \) sufficiently large.
- You may use standard inequalities and comparison arguments.

## Constraints

- The proof must be based on the definition of the Laplace transform.
- Integration by parts must be shown explicitly.
- Growth conditions must be clearly stated and used.
- No reliance on transform tables.
