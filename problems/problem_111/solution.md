# Solution

## Method
Assuming zero-initial conditions and applying the Laplace transform

\[
m{s}^{2}X\left( s\right)  + {bsX}\left( s\right)  + {kX}\left( s\right)  = F\left( s\right)
\]

from which

\[
X\left( s\right)  = G\left( s\right) F\left( s\right) ,\;G\left( s\right)  = \frac{1}{k}\frac{{\omega }_{n}^{2}}{{s}^{2} + {2\zeta }{\omega }_{n}s + {\omega }_{n}^{2}},\;{\omega }_{n} = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}},\;\zeta  = \frac{b}{2\sqrt{mk}}
\]

## Teaching Points
1. Conversion of mechanical differential equations to transfer functions.
2. Role of zero initial conditions in Laplace analysis.
3. Canonical form of second-order transfer functions.
4. Physical interpretation of natural frequency and damping ratio.
5. Relationship between positive parameters and system stability.
6. Asymptotic stability determined by left-half-plane poles.
