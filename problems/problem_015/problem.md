# Problem: Coupled Rotational Inertias with Viscous Friction

## Problem Description
Redo P2.10 in the presence of viscous friction torques, \( - {b}_{1}{\omega }_{1} \) and \( - {b}_{2}{\omega }_{2} \) , on each inertia to obtain the differential equation

\[
\left( {{J}_{1}{r}_{2}^{2} + {J}_{2}{r}_{1}^{2}}\right) {\dot{\omega }}_{1} + \left( {{b}_{1}{r}_{2}^{2} + {b}_{2}{r}_{1}^{2}}\right) {\omega }_{1} = {r}_{2}^{2}\tau .
\]

## Subproblems
1. Write the rotational equations of motion for inertia \( J_1 \) including viscous friction.
2. Write the rotational equations of motion for inertia \( J_2 \) including viscous friction.
3. Use the no-slip belt constraint to relate \( \omega_1 \) and \( \omega_2 \).
4. Eliminate the internal belt forces from the equations.
5. Derive a single differential equation describing the dynamics of \( \omega_1 \) as a function of the applied torque \( \tau \).

---

## Additional Information
- The belt is assumed to be inextensible and massless.
- No slip occurs between the belt and the pulleys.
- Radii of the pulleys attached to \( J_1 \) and \( J_2 \) are \( r_1 \) and \( r_2 \), respectively.
- Internal belt forces are denoted by \( f_1 \) and \( f_2 \).
- All parameters are positive constants.

---

## Constraints
- All governing equations must be derived using Newton’s second law for rotation.
- Clearly state and apply kinematic constraints due to the belt.
- Internal forces must be eliminated to obtain a reduced-order model.
- The final result must be expressed as a single differential equation.
