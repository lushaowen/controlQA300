# Problem

## Problem Description
Consider the simplified model of the rotating machine described. Calculate the machine’s angular position, \( {\theta }_{2}\left( t\right) \) , obtained in response to a constant-torque input, \( \tau \left( t\right)  = \widetilde{\tau }, t \geq  0 \) . Identify the transient and the steady-state components of the response.

## Subproblems
1. Express the constant-torque input \( \tau(t) = \widetilde{\tau} \) in the Laplace domain.
2. Identify the transfer function \( H(s) \) relating torque input to angular position.
3. Compute the Laplace-domain angular position response \( \Theta_2(s) \).
4. Perform partial-fraction expansion of \( \Theta_2(s) \).
5. Apply inverse Laplace transformation to obtain \( \theta_2(t) \).
6. Identify the exponentially decaying transient component.
7. Identify the steady-state component and describe its time dependence.
8. Interpret the physical meaning of a linearly growing steady-state term.

## Additional Information
- Assume zero initial conditions.
- Parameters \( \alpha \) and \( \beta \) are defined as in Problem P3.54.
- The rotating machine model is linear and time-invariant.
- Standard Laplace transform pairs for polynomial and exponential terms apply.

## Constraints
- All derivations must follow Laplace transform theory.
- Intermediate symbolic expressions must remain symbolic.
- Transient and steady-state components must be clearly separated.
- Physical interpretation should be consistent with rotational mechanics.
