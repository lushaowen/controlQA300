# Problem
## Problem Description
 Change variables under the Laplace integral to prove the frequency-shift property in Table 3.2.

![alt text](images\image.png)
## Subproblems

1. Write the Laplace transform of the exponentially weighted function.
2. Combine exponential terms inside the integral.
3. Interpret the resulting expression as a Laplace transform.
4. Identify the corresponding shift in the complex frequency variable.
5. Explain the meaning of this property in the Laplace domain.

## Additional Information

- Assume \( f(t) \) is of exponential order.
- Let \( s = \beta + j\gamma \) with \( \beta \) sufficiently large.
- The constant \( a \) may be positive or negative.

## Constraints

- The derivation must start from the integral definition of the Laplace transform.
- No use of transform tables.
- The result must be obtained through algebraic manipulation of the integral.
