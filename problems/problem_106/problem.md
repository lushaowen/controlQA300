# Problem

## Problem Description
 the sinusoidal torque input \( \tau \left( t\right)  = \widetilde{\tau }\cos \left( {\omega t}\right) , t \geq  0 \) .

## Subproblems
1. Express the given sinusoidal torque input in the Laplace domain under zero initial conditions.
2. Determine the transfer function relationship between the applied torque and the angular displacement \( \Theta_2(s) \).
3. Identify and explain any pole-zero cancellations that occur in the Laplace-domain expression.
4. Perform partial fraction expansion of the resulting transfer function.
5. Compute the inverse Laplace transform to obtain the time-domain response \( \theta_2(t) \).
6. Decompose the response into transient and steady-state components.

## Additional Information
- The system is assumed to be linear and time-invariant (LTI).
- Zero initial conditions are assumed throughout the analysis.
- Parameters \( \alpha \) and \( \beta \) are defined as in Problem P3.54.
- Standard Laplace transform pairs and partial fraction techniques may be used.
- Complex exponential representations may be employed for sinusoidal terms.

## Constraints
- All steps must be justified using Laplace-domain analysis.
- Final results should clearly distinguish between transient and steady-state behavior.
- Mathematical expressions should be simplified where possible.
- The physical interpretation of the response components should be consistent with system dynamics.
