# Solution

## Method

In SI units, \( v\left( t\right) \) is in \( \mathrm{m}/\mathrm{s} \) and \( \dot{v}\left( t\right) \) is in \( \mathrm{m}/{\mathrm{s}}^{2} \), and \( m\dot{v} \) and \( {bv}\left( t\right) \) are in \( \mathrm{N} = \mathrm{kg}\,\mathrm{m}/{\mathrm{s}}^{2} \), \( m \) is in \( \mathrm{kg} \) and \( b \) is in \( \mathrm{kg}/\mathrm{s} \). Therefore \( \lambda = - b/m \) is in \( \mathrm{s}^{-1} \) and \( \tau = - \lambda^{-1} \) is in \( \mathrm{s} \).

2. Physical interpretation of model parameters in dynamic systems
3. Relationship between system parameters and time constants
4. Understanding exponential system responses through units
5. Connection between mathematical solutions and physical behavior
