# Problem: Step Response of an Op-Amp RC Circuit

## Problem Description

Consider the operational amplifier (Op-Amp) circuit introduced previously, consisting of a resistor–capacitor network connected to an ideal Op-Amp.  
The circuit parameters are given as:

\[
R_1 = 1\,\text{M}\Omega, \quad C_1 = C_2 = 10\,\mu\text{F}
\]

Assume that all initial conditions are zero. A constant input voltage

\[
v(t) = 10\,\text{V}, \quad t \ge 0
\]

is applied to the circuit.

The goal is to analyze the time-domain response of the output voltage \( v_0(t) \).

---

## Subproblems

1. Using the circuit’s differential equation, derive an explicit expression for the output voltage \( v_0(t) \).
2. Evaluate the integral terms under the assumption of constant input voltage.
3. Determine whether the output voltage is bounded or unbounded.
4. Sketch the qualitative time evolution of \( v_0(t) \) for \( t \ge 0 \).
5. Interpret the physical meaning of the response shape.

---

## Additional Information

- The operational amplifier is assumed to be ideal (infinite gain).
- Capacitors are ideal and linear.
- Zero initial conditions apply to all state variables.
- Only time-domain analysis is required.

---

## Constraints

- Do not use Laplace transforms.
- Show all intermediate steps.
- Express the final solution explicitly as a function of time.
- The sketch may be qualitative (not to scale).
