# Problem

## Problem Description
Use the differentiation property for the first-order derivative to formally prove the time differentiation property in Table 3.2 for higher-order derivatives.

![alt text](images\image.png)
## Subproblems

1. Express the second derivative of \( f(t) \) in terms of the derivative of a new function.
2. Apply the first-order differentiation property to this new function.
3. Substitute the Laplace transform of the first derivative of \( f(t) \).
4. Derive the explicit expression for the Laplace transform of the second derivative.
5. Generalize the result to higher-order derivatives.

## Additional Information

- The notation \( 0^- \) allows for possible discontinuities at \( t = 0 \).
- All derivatives of \( f(t) \) are assumed to be piecewise continuous.
- Assume the real part of \( s \) is sufficiently large to ensure convergence.

## Constraints

- The proof must rely on the first-order differentiation property.
- All intermediate steps must be shown.
- Initial conditions must be clearly identified.
- No use of Laplace transform tables.
