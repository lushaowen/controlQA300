# Problem

## Problem Description
the sinusoidal torque input \( \tau \left( t\right)  = \widetilde{\tau }\cos \left( {\omega t}\right) , t \geq  0 \) .

## Subproblems
1. Express the sinusoidal torque input \( \tau(t) = \widetilde{\tau}\cos(\omega t) \) in the Laplace domain.
2. Write the transfer function \( G(s) \) of the rotating machine as defined in P3.54.
3. Compute the Laplace-domain angular velocity response \( \Omega_2(s) \).
4. Perform partial-fraction expansion of \( \Omega_2(s) \) to enable inverse Laplace transformation.
5. Compute the inverse Laplace transform to obtain \( \omega_2(t) \).
6. Identify the exponentially decaying transient term.
7. Identify the steady-state sinusoidal response and its dependence on system parameters.
8. Interpret the frequency response characteristics implied by the steady-state solution.

## Additional Information
- Assume zero initial conditions.
- Parameters \( \alpha \) and \( \beta \) are defined as in Problem P3.54.
- The system is linear and time-invariant.
- Complex-frequency partial fractions may be used.
- Standard Laplace transform pairs for sinusoidal functions apply.

## Constraints
- All derivations must be consistent with Laplace transform theory.
- Complex-valued intermediate expressions must be handled correctly.
- The final result must clearly separate transient and steady-state components.
- Symbolic parameters should remain symbolic throughout.
