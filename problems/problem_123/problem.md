# Problem

## Problem Description

Consider the simplified model of the DC motor described. Calculate the motor’s angular velocity, \( \omega(t) \), obtained in response to a constant-armature-voltage input,
\( v_{\mathrm{a}}(t) = \widetilde{v}_{\mathrm{a}},\ t \geq 0 \).
Identify the transient and the steady-state components of the response.

## Subproblems

1. Represent the constant armature voltage input in the Laplace domain.
2. Write the transfer-function \( G(s) \) from armature voltage to angular velocity.
3. Compute the Laplace transform of the angular velocity \( \Omega(s) \).
4. Perform partial fraction expansion of \( \Omega(s) \).
5. Compute the inverse Laplace transform to obtain \( \omega(t) \).
6. Identify and interpret the transient and steady-state components of the response.

## Additional Information

- The DC motor is modeled as a linear time-invariant system.
- Initial conditions are assumed to be zero.
- Parameters \( \alpha \) and \( \beta \) are positive constants defined by the motor model.
- Standard Laplace transform techniques are applicable.

## Constraints

- The solution must be obtained using Laplace-domain analysis.
- Partial fraction expansion must be explicitly shown.
- Transient and steady-state terms must be clearly identified.
- Final expressions should be physically interpretable.
