# Problem: Frequency Differentiation Property of the Laplace Transform

## Problem Description
Prove the frequency differentiation property in Table 3.2. Hint: Calculate \( {F}^{\prime }\left( s\right) \) by differentiating inside the Laplace integral.

![alt text](images\image.png)
## Subproblems

1. Write the Laplace transform \( F(s) \) using its integral definition.
2. Differentiate \( F(s) \) with respect to \( s \).
3. Justify the interchange of differentiation and integration.
4. Simplify the resulting expression.
5. Interpret the result in terms of a Laplace transform of a time-weighted function.
6. Comment on how higher-order derivatives with respect to \( s \) can be obtained.

## Additional Information

- Assume \( f(t) \) is piecewise continuous and of exponential order.
- Let \( s = \beta + j\gamma \) with \( \beta \) sufficiently large.
- Standard results from calculus on differentiation under the integral sign may be used.

## Constraints

- The derivation must begin from the definition of the Laplace transform.
- All differentiation steps must be shown explicitly.
- No use of Laplace transform tables.
- The final result must be expressed using Laplace transform notation.
