# Solution

## Method

Because there are no pole-zero cancelations in the product \( GK \) and

\[
S = \frac{1}{1 + GK} = \frac{1}{1 + \frac{1}{s}} = \frac{s}{s + 1}
\]

has a pole at \( s = -1 \), \( S \) is asymptotically stable and the closed-loop is internally stable. Because \( G \) has a pole at \( s = 0 \) and \( S \) has a zero at \( s = 0 \), the closed-loop achieves asymptotic tracking of a constant input.

## Teaching Points

1. Definition and significance of internal stability
2. Use of the sensitivity function in stability analysis
3. Relationship between integrators and steady-state tracking
4. Importance of pole-zero locations in feedback systems
5. Distinction between stability and performance objectives
