# Problem

## Problem Description
 An (inextensible and massless) belt is used to drive a rotating machine without slip as shown in Fig. 2.18(a). The simplified motion of the inertia \( {J}_{1} \) is described by

\[
{J}_{1}{\dot{\omega }}_{1} = \tau  + {f}_{1}{r}_{1} - {f}_{2}{r}_{1},
\]

where \( \tau \) is the torque applied by the driving motor and \( {f}_{1} \) and \( {f}_{2} \) are tensions on the belt. The machine is connected to the inertia \( {J}_{2} \) , which represents the sum of the inertias of all machine parts. The motion of the inertia \( {J}_{2} \) is described by

\[
{J}_{2}{\dot{\omega }}_{2} = {f}_{2}{r}_{2} - {f}_{1}{r}_{2}.
\]



Show that the motion of the entire system can be described by the differential equation

\[
\left( {{J}_{1}{r}_{2}^{2} + {J}_{2}{r}_{1}^{2}}\right) {\dot{\omega }}_{1} = {r}_{2}^{2}\tau ,\;{\omega }_{2} = \left( {{r}_{1}/{r}_{2}}\right) {\omega }_{1}.
\]

## Subproblems

1. Determine the kinematic relationship between \( \omega_1 \) and \( \omega_2 \).
2. Eliminate the belt tensions \( f_1 \) and \( f_2 \) from the equations of motion.
3. Derive a single differential equation describing the motion of the entire system in terms of \( \omega_1 \).
4. Identify the equivalent inertia seen by the driving motor.

---

## Additional Information
- Linear belt speed must be identical at both pulleys.
- The system is purely rotational but coupled through translational belt motion.
- The result should be expressed as an effective inertia multiplying \( \dot{\omega}_1 \).

---

## Constraints
- Show all intermediate steps clearly.
- Use consistent sign conventions.
- Final expressions must be physically interpretable.
- Use symbolic parameters \( J_1, J_2, r_1, r_2 \).
