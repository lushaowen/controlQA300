# Solution

## Method

Note that

\[
\widehat{K} = \frac{K}{1 + \widetilde{G}K},
\]

and that the poles of \( \widetilde{G} \) are the poles of \( G \).

Because the zeros of \( (1 + \widetilde{G}K)^{-1} \) are the poles of \( \widetilde{G}K \), that is the poles of \( GK \), the zeros of

\[
\widehat{K} = K(1 + \widetilde{G}K)^{-1}
\]

are the poles of \( G \).

## Teaching Points

1. Structural relationships between poles and zeros in feedback systems.
2. Interpretation of modified controllers in loop-shaping theory.
3. Role of pole-zero cancellations in system analysis.
4. Use of algebraic transfer-function manipulation for theoretical proofs.
5. Connection between plant dynamics and controller zero locations.
