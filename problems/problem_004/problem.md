# Problem: First-Order Taylor Expansion of a Nonlinear Function

## Problem Description
Compute the first-order Taylor series expansion of the function $y\left( u\right)  = \; \alpha {\tan }^{-1}\left( {\beta u}\right)$ about $u = 0$.
## Subproblems
1. Compute the Taylor series expansion of $y(u)$ about $u = 0$.
2. Identify the linear (first-order) term in the expansion.
3. Determine the slope of $y(u)$ at $u = 0$.
4. Explain how this slope corresponds to the value shown in Fig. 1.6.

## Additional Information
- Assume $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are real constants.
- Use standard Taylor series expansions.
- Higher-order terms may be neglected for small values of $u$.


## Constraints
- All derivations must be shown.
- Use analytical differentiation.
- Clearly state the order of approximation.
