# Solution

## Method

This is a linear model for which we can calculate the solution:

\[
x\left( t\right)  = {e}^{rt}x\left( 0\right) ,
\]

\[
r = b - m.
\]

When the birth rate, \( r \) , is larger then the mortality rate, \( m \) , then the population grows exponentially. Otherwise, it decays exponentially.

## Teaching Points

1. Solution of first-order linear differential equations
2. Interpretation of exponential growth and decay
3. Relationship between model parameters and system behavior
4. Identification of equilibrium conditions in population models
5. Limitations of exponential growth assumptions
