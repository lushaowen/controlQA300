# Solution

## Method

Use the approximation \( {\omega }_{\mathrm{d}}t \approx  {2k\pi }, k \in  \mathbb{Z} \) , formula (6.6), and solve for \( y\left( {t}_{s}\right)  - 1 \approx \) 0.02 to establish the settling time approximate formula (6.10).

## Teaching Points

1. Definition and practical interpretation of settling time in control systems.
2. Use of envelope approximations for oscillatory transient responses.
3. Justification of neglecting sinusoidal variations in settling time analysis.
4. Connection between exponential decay rate and system damping.
5. Understanding approximation-based performance metrics in control design.
