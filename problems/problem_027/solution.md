## Teaching Points

1. Proper choice of reference variables can greatly simplify system equations
2. Physical laws remain invariant, but mathematical form depends on coordinate selection
3. Rest lengths and equilibrium positions can often be eliminated by redefining variables
4. Linear system models describe deviations around equilibrium points
5. Modeling choices affect clarity but not underlying physics
