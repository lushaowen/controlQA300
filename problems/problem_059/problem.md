# Problem: First Differentiation Property of the Laplace Transform

## Problem Description
Assume that the function \( f \) has exponential growth and that the Laplace transform of \( \dot{f} \) exists. Prove the first differentiation property in Table 3.2 for the first derivative. Hint: Evaluate the integral in \( \mathcal{L}\{ \dot{f}\left( t\right) \} \) by parts.

![alt text](images\image.png)
## Subproblems

1. Write the Laplace transform of \( \dot{f}(t) \) using its integral definition.
2. Apply integration by parts to the integral.
3. Identify the boundary term resulting from integration by parts.
4. Use the exponential growth condition to evaluate the boundary term.
5. Derive the final differentiation property formula.

## Additional Information

- The notation \( 0^- \) allows for possible discontinuities at \( t = 0 \).
- Assume \( s = \beta + j\gamma \) with \( \beta \) sufficiently large.
- The function \( f(t) \) is assumed to be piecewise continuous.

## Constraints

- The proof must be based on the definition of the Laplace transform.
- Integration by parts must be carried out explicitly.
- All assumptions must be clearly stated.
- No use of transform tables.
