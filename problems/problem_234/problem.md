# Problem

## Problem Description

 you have designed the spring and damper on the one-eighth-car model from  for a car with \( 1/4 \) mass equal to \( 640\,\mathrm{kg} \) to have a natural frequency \( f_{\mathrm{n}} = 2.5\,\mathrm{Hz} \) and damping ratio \( \zeta = 0.08 \). Draw the Bode magnitude and phase diagrams corresponding to the design in P6.20. Interpret your result using Bode plots and the frequency-response method.

## Subproblems

1. Write down the transfer function of the one-eighth-car model used in the design.
2. Identify the locations of the system poles and zeros.
3. Sketch or compute the Bode magnitude and phase plots of the transfer function.
4. Determine the resonant frequency and explain its relationship to the natural frequency.
5. Compute the peak magnitude of the frequency response.
6. Explain the effect of damping ratio on the resonance peak.
7. Interpret the high-frequency and low-frequency asymptotic behavior.
8. Relate the Bode plot features to ride comfort and vehicle dynamics.

## Additional Information

- The one-eighth-car model represents vertical vehicle dynamics.
- The transfer function corresponds to acceleration response due to road excitation.
- The system is linear and time-invariant.
- Frequency-domain analysis is used to assess resonance and vibration amplification.

## Constraints

- Use frequency-response and Bode plot methods only.
- Interpret results qualitatively and quantitatively.
- Physical interpretation must accompany mathematical analysis.
- Numerical values should be consistent with the design in P6.20.
