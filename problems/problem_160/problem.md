# Problem

## Problem Description

Verify that the linearized model of a steering car, Equations (5.23), developed in Section is not controllable. Interpret this result on the basis of your knowledge of the physical behavior of this system. Hint: Use P5.3.

## Subproblems

1. Write down the state-space representation of the linearized steering car model.
2. Identify the system matrix and input matrix.
3. Determine the eigenvalues relevant to controllability analysis.
4. Verify the uncontrollability of the system using a suitable controllability condition.
5. Interpret the uncontrollable mode in terms of the physical motion of the vehicle.

## Additional Information

- The system is obtained by linearizing the nonlinear steering car model around straight-line motion.
- The vehicle is assumed to move with constant forward velocity.
- The input corresponds to the steering angle.
- Small-angle and small-deviation assumptions apply.
- Reference is made to the controllability concepts discussed in Problem P5.3.

## Constraints

- Use linear systems theory for the controllability analysis.
- Arguments must be consistent with physical intuition.
- No nonlinear control techniques are considered.
- The analysis is local around the operating point.
- Mathematical verification must be accompanied by physical interpretation.
