# Problem

## Problem Description

Consider the mass–spring–damper system whose motion is described by the differential equation:
\[
m\ddot{x} + b\dot{x} + kx = mg\sin\theta
\]

Define a new variable:
\[
y = x - k^{-1}mg\sin\theta
\]

1. Rewrite the equation of motion in terms of the new variable \( y \).
2. Show that the resulting equation is homogeneous.
3. Relate this transformation to a different choice of the reference position \( x_0 \).
4. Comment on the effect of constant forces on the dynamic behavior of the system.

---

## Subproblems

1. Express \( x \), \( \dot{x} \), and \( \ddot{x} \) in terms of \( y \).
2. Substitute the transformed variables into the original equation.
3. Simplify the resulting expression to obtain the equation of motion in \( y \).
4. Interpret the physical meaning of the transformation.
5. Explain how gravity affects the static equilibrium but not the dynamics.

---

## Additional Information

- The system is linear and time-invariant.
- The gravitational force component \( mg\sin\theta \) is constant.
- The spring and damper are linear.
- The transformation represents a shift in the equilibrium position.

---

## Constraints

- Show all algebraic steps clearly.
- Use analytical reasoning only.
- Provide both mathematical and physical interpretations.
- Assume all parameters are positive constants.
