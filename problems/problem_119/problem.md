# Problem

## Problem Description
Consider the model of the circuit described. Calculate the capacitor voltage, \( {v}_{C}\left( t\right) \), obtained in response to a constant input voltage, \( v\left( t\right)  = \widetilde{v}, t \geq  0 \). Identify the transient and the steady-state components of the response. How do the parameters \( R, L \), and \( C \) affect the response?

## Subproblems
1. Express the constant input voltage in the Laplace domain.
2. Recall the transfer function of the RLC circuit from P3.85.
3. Compute the Laplace transform of the capacitor voltage.
4. Rewrite the result to explicitly separate steady-state and transient terms.
5. Identify the steady-state component in the time domain.
6. Describe the form of the transient response.
7. Explain how the parameters \( R \), \( L \), and \( C \) influence the transient dynamics and response speed.

## Additional Information
- The circuit is modeled as a second-order linear time-invariant system.
- Zero initial conditions are assumed.
- A constant input corresponds to a step input in the Laplace domain.
- The steady-state response is determined by the DC gain of the transfer function.
- The transient response depends on the natural frequency and damping ratio.

## Constraints
- All circuit parameters \( R \), \( L \), and \( C \) are positive.
- Laplace transform methods must be used.
- The response should be expressed in a form that clearly separates transient and steady-state components.
- Parameter effects should be interpreted using standard second-order system concepts.
