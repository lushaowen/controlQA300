# Solution

## Method

The mechanical power is \( {\tau \omega } = {K}_{t}{i}_{a}\omega \) and the electrical power is \( {v}_{e}{i}_{a} = {K}_{e}\omega {i}_{a} \), therefore \( {K}_{t} = {K}_{e} \).

## Teaching Points

1. Power conservation in electromechanical systems
2. Physical interpretation of DC motor constants
3. Relationship between torque production and back-emf generation
4. Importance of ideal modeling assumptions
5. Connection between electrical and mechanical domains
