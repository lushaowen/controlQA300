# Problem

## Problem Description
Consider the simplified model of the rotating machine described. Calculate the machine’s angular velocity, \( {\omega }_{2}\left( t\right) \) , obtained in response to a constant-torque input, \( \tau \left( t\right)  = \widetilde{\tau }, t \geq  0 \) . Identify the transient and the steady-state components of the response.

## Subproblems
1. Express the constant-torque input \( \tau(t) = \widetilde{\tau} \) in the Laplace domain.
2. Determine the transfer function \( G(s) \) relating input torque to angular velocity for the rotating machine model.
3. Compute the Laplace-domain expression for the angular velocity response \( \Omega_2(s) \).
4. Perform partial-fraction expansion to prepare for inverse Laplace transformation.
5. Compute the time-domain response \( \omega_2(t) \).
6. Separate the total response into transient and steady-state components.
7. Interpret the physical meaning of the transient decay rate and steady-state value.

## Additional Information
- Assume zero initial conditions.
- The rotating machine model parameters \( \alpha \) and \( \beta \) are defined as in Problem P3.54.
- The system is assumed to be linear and time-invariant.
- The Laplace transform tables referenced correspond to Appendix C.

## Constraints
- All steps must be consistent with Laplace transform theory.
- Intermediate algebraic steps should be clearly justified.
- The final answer must explicitly identify transient and steady-state terms.
- Symbolic parameters should not be replaced with numerical values.
