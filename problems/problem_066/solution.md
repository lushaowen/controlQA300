## Teaching Points

1. Time integration in the time domain corresponds to division by \( s \) in the Laplace domain.
2. The final value theorem links steady-state behavior to low-frequency limits.
3. Inverse tangent limits encode sign information naturally.
4. This result is fundamental in signal processing and system theory.
5. Laplace transforms provide an elegant alternative to direct real analysis methods.
