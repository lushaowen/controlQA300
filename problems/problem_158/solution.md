# Solution

## Method
 All \( \lambda \) and \( x \neq  0 \) such that \( \left( {A - {\lambda I}}\right) x \) are given by

\[
\left\lbrack  \begin{matrix} \lambda &  - 1 \\  0 & \lambda  \end{matrix}\right\rbrack  \left( \begin{array}{l} {x}_{1} \\  {x}_{2} \end{array}\right)  = 0\; \Rightarrow  \;\lambda  = 0,\;\left( \begin{array}{l} {x}_{1} \\  {x}_{2} \end{array}\right)  = \left( \begin{array}{l} 1 \\  0 \end{array}\right)
\]

Therefore

\[
0 = {Cx} = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{ll} \alpha & 1 \end{array}\right\rbrack  \left( \begin{array}{l} 1 \\  0 \end{array}\right)  = \alpha
\]

only when \( \alpha  = 0 \) .
