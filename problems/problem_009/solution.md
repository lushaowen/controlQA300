# Solution

## Method
With Newton's second law help we write

\[
m\dot{v} = F = {mg} - {bv}
\]

or \( m\dot{v} + {bv} = {mg} \) .



## Teaching Points
1. Newton’s second law links physical forces to differential equations
2. Linear drag leads to first-order dynamics
3. Modeling assumptions directly affect equation structure
4. Sign conventions are essential for correct formulation
