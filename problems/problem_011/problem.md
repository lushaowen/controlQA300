# Problem: Dynamic Response of a First-Order Velocity Model

## Problem Description
The first-order ordinary differential equation obtained in P2.4 can be seen as a dynamic system where the output is the vertical velocity, \( v \) , and the input is the gravitational force, \( {mg} \) . Calculate the solution to this equation. Consider \( m = 1\mathrm{\;{kg}}, b = {10}\mathrm{\;{kg}}/\mathrm{s} \) , \( g = {10}\mathrm{\;m}/{\mathrm{s}}^{2} \) . Sketch or use MATLAB to plot the response, \( v\left( t\right) \) , when \( v\left( 0\right)  = 0, v\left( 0\right)  = \; 1\mathrm{\;m}/\mathrm{s} \) , or \( v\left( 0\right)  =  - 1\mathrm{\;m}/\mathrm{s} \) .

## Subproblems
1. Solve the first-order differential equation for $v(t)$.
2. Identify the steady-state velocity.
3. Evaluate the solution for the given numerical parameters.
4. Analyze the effect of different initial conditions.

## Assumptions
- Linear air resistance.
- Constant gravitational acceleration.
- Motion is strictly vertical.
- Parameters are time-invariant.

## Constraints
- Use the model derived in P2.4.
- Express the solution explicitly in time.
- Clearly indicate steady-state behavior.
