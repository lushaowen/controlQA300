# Problem

## Problem Description

Show that the roots of the second-order polynomial

\[
{s}^{2} + {2\zeta }{\omega }_{\mathrm{n}}s - {\omega }_{\mathrm{n}}^{2} = 0,\;{\omega }_{\mathrm{n}} > 0,
\]

are always real and at least one of the roots is positive.

## Subproblems

1. Compute the discriminant of the given second-order polynomial.
2. Analyze the sign of the discriminant for all real values of the damping ratio \( \zeta \).
3. Derive the explicit expressions for the two roots of the polynomial.
4. Determine the conditions under which the roots are real.
5. Prove that at least one of the roots is strictly positive for all real \( \zeta \).

## Additional Information

- The parameter \( \omega_n \) represents a positive natural frequency.
- The parameter \( \zeta \) is a real-valued damping ratio.
- The polynomial resembles the characteristic equation of a second-order dynamical system with negative stiffness.
- Standard results from quadratic equations and real analysis may be used.

## Constraints

- The proof must be valid for all real values of \( \zeta \).
- Algebraic manipulations should be shown explicitly.
- Inequalities used in the argument must be justified.
- The reasoning should not rely on numerical examples.
