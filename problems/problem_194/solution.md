# Solution

## Method

\[
{s}_{1/2} = {\omega }_{n}\left( {-\zeta  \pm  \sqrt{{\zeta }^{2} + 1}}\right) ,
\]

which by \( \left( {{\zeta }^{2} + 1}\right)  > 0 \) is always real. Furthermore, at least one root is positive because \( \sqrt{{\zeta }^{2} + 1} > \zeta \) .

## Teaching Points

1. Application of the quadratic formula to analyze characteristic equations.
2. Interpretation of the discriminant in determining the nature of polynomial roots.
3. Use of inequality reasoning to establish positivity of system eigenvalues.
4. Understanding how system parameters influence stability-related properties.
5. Recognition that real roots do not necessarily imply system stability.
