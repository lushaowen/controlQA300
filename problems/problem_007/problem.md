# Problem: Justification of a Linear Approximation Using Taylor Series

## Problem Description
Use the Taylor series expansion of the function \( f\left( x\right)  = {\tan }^{-1}\left( x\right) \) to justify the approximation

\[
c\tan \left( {{\alpha }^{-1}y}\right)  \approx  {by}
\]

when \( b = {\alpha }^{-1}c \) .

## Subproblems
1. Write the Taylor series expansion of $\tan^{-1}(x)$ about $x = 0$.
2. Substitute $x = \alpha^{-1} y$ into the expansion.
3. Multiply the result by the constant $c$.
4. Identify the linear approximation and justify the choice of $b$.

## Additional Information
- Assume $y$ is sufficiently small.
- Higher-order terms may be neglected.
- All constants are real and finite.

## Constraints
- The Taylor series expansion must be explicitly stated.
- The order of neglected terms must be indicated.
- The approximation must be clearly justified.
