# Solution
## Method
About \( x = 0 \) the function \( f\left( x\right)  = {\tan }^{-1}\left( x\right) \) admits the Taylor Series expansion \( f\left( x\right)  = x + O\left( {x}^{3}\right) \) hence

\[
c{\tan }^{-1}\left( {{\alpha }^{-1}y}\right)  = c{\alpha }^{-1}y + O\left( {y}^{3}\right)  \approx  c{\alpha }^{-1}y = {by}.
\]

## Teaching Points
1. Taylor series provide a systematic method for linearizing nonlinear functions
2. Small-signal approximations rely on neglecting higher-order terms
3. Parameter grouping simplifies expressions and reveals effective gains
4. Validity of approximations depends on the operating region
