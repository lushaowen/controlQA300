# Problem

## Problem Description
the sinusoidal voltage input \( v\left( t\right)  = \widetilde{v}\cos \left( {\omega t}\right) , t \geq  0 \).

## Subproblems
1. Express the sinusoidal input voltage in the Laplace domain.
2. Recall the transfer function of the RC circuit from P3.80.
3. Compute the Laplace transform of the capacitor voltage.
4. Perform partial fraction expansion to separate transient and sinusoidal terms.
5. Compute the inverse Laplace transform to obtain the time-domain response.
6. Identify and distinguish the transient and steady-state components of the response.
7. Express the steady-state response in terms of amplitude scaling and phase shift.

## Additional Information
- The circuit is modeled as a first-order linear time-invariant system.
- Zero initial conditions are assumed.
- A sinusoidal input corresponds to excitation at a single frequency \( \omega \).
- The steady-state response is determined by the frequency response of the system.
- The transient response decays exponentially due to circuit damping.

## Constraints
- All physical parameters are positive.
- Laplace transform and inverse Laplace transform methods must be used.
- The final answer should clearly separate transient and steady-state terms.
- Trigonometric expressions should be simplified where possible.
