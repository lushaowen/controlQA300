# Solution

## Method
When \( \bar{u} = {mg}\sin \theta \) , equilibrium is

\( \left( \begin{array}{l} 0 \\  0 \end{array}\right)  = \left\lbrack  \begin{matrix}  - \frac{b}{m} &  - \frac{k}{m} \\  1 & 0 \end{matrix}\right\rbrack  \left( \begin{array}{l} {x}_{1} \\  {x}_{2} \end{array}\right)  + \left\lbrack  \begin{matrix} \frac{1}{m} \\  0 \end{matrix}\right\rbrack  {mg}\sin \theta \; {x}_{1} = 0 \) , \( {x}_{2} = \left( {{mg}/k}\right) \sin \theta \)

The state-space equations about equilibrium are as inwith \( A, B, C, D \) asand

\[
\widetilde{x} = \left( \begin{matrix} \dot{x} \\  x - \left( {{mg}/k}\right) \sin \theta  \end{matrix}\right) ,
\]

which corresponds to the change of coordinates.
