# Solution

## Methon
With Newton's second law help we write

\[
m\ddot{x} =  - b\dot{x} - {k}_{1}\left( {x + {x}_{0} - {\ell }_{0,1}}\right)  + {k}_{2}\left( {d - x - {x}_{0} - w - {\ell }_{0,2}}\right)
\]

\[
=  - b\dot{x} - \left( {{k}_{1} + {k}_{2}}\right) x - {k}_{1}\left( {{x}_{0} - {\ell }_{0,1}}\right)  + {k}_{2}\left( {d - {x}_{0} - w - {\ell }_{0,2}}\right)
\]

\[
=  - b\dot{x} - \left( {{k}_{1} + {k}_{2}}\right) x
\]

after the choice

\[
{x}_{0} = \frac{{k}_{1}{\ell }_{0,1} + {k}_{2}\left( {d - w - {\ell }_{0,2}}\right) }{{k}_{1} + {k}_{2}}.
\]

When \( d \geq  w + {\ell }_{0,1} + {\ell }_{0,2} \) both springs are stretched so that the mass experiences tensile forces. When \( d \leq \; w + {\ell }_{0,1} + {\ell }_{0,2} \) both springs are compressed so that the mass experiences compressive forces.

## Teaching Points

1. Multiple springs in opposition combine into an equivalent stiffness
2. Equilibrium position absorbs geometric and rest-length information
3. Dynamic behavior depends on stiffness, mass, and damping—not absolute dimensions
4. Tensile and compressive preload affect equilibrium but not linear dynamics
5. Reference position choice is critical for simplified modeling
